---
eaves Fairfax City is an AvalonBay community at 10608 Kitty Pozer Drive, rated about 4.3 out of 5 from roughly 232 reviews. Rent runs roughly $2,245 to $3,410 per month.

What residents praise:
- Quick maintenance response times and friendly staff; the office signs for packages. One staff member, Mr. George Conger, is repeatedly praised for professionalism and attentiveness.
- Spacious, thoughtfully designed apartment layouts.
- No difficulty finding parking.
- Community amenities including a pool, playground, and tennis court.

What residents complain about:
- Noise is the biggest complaint. Residents describe neighbors loud past midnight, to the point that "beds shake," and say little concrete action is taken. Management says it can only enforce noise complaints during posted quiet hours (10pm-8am) and cannot handle complaints that occur after the office closes (5-6pm).
- Value: several residents consider it overpriced for the area, citing no garage parking, no fitness center, and significant rent increases after the first year.
- Maintenance can be slow for some work, with residents waiting weeks for tasks like paint to be completed.
- Some residents also raise pest-control concerns.
