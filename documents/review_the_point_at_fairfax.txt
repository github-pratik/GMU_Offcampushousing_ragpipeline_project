TOPIC: Resident experiences at The Point at Fairfax (4040 Gateway Drive)
---
The Point at Fairfax is a newer luxury community at 4040 Gateway Drive, with rent roughly $1,928 to $3,027 per month. It is managed by Pantzer, which residents note has been ranked #1 among top management companies by SatisFacts and ApartmentRatings.

What residents praise:
- Management and staff: Community Manager Laura is repeatedly singled out as the "glue which holds the community together," greeting residents by name and being friendly and approachable.
- Maintenance is described as top notch, with many service requests completed within a day.
- Modern furnishings and a convenient location near grocery stores and food options.

What residents complain about:
- Cleanliness of common areas, especially the parking garage and trash disposal areas.
- Parking is limited, partly due to nearby businesses. Additional parking spots cost about $100 per month and reserved parking about $125 per month.
- Construction quality: paper-thin walls let noise travel easily between units.
- Communication: residents report fire alarms going off randomly without prior notice, and frustration with how management communicates about such issues.
