TOPIC: The Flats on University and The Main on University — student-focused apartments near GMU
---
Two purpose-built student communities market directly to George Mason University students. Note: these descriptions come from the properties' own marketing and listings; few independent resident reviews were available, so treat amenity claims as advertised rather than verified by tenants.

The Flats on University:
- Studio through 4-bedroom off-campus apartments, located just north of George Mason University.
- Modern furnishings, in-unit laundry, and stress-free roommate matching.
- Amenities include a resort-style pool and a fitness center.
- About a 22-minute walk to campus, and offers free CUE bus rides to GMU.

The Main on University:
- Luxury one, two, and four-bedroom homes in Fairfax, marketed as "steps from" George Mason University.
- Fully furnished with student-oriented amenities.
- Residents can ride free to GMU on the CUE bus.
