Commute and transit:
- The CUE bus offers free transportation connecting George Mason University and the Fairfax area, with bus stops typically within about a 2-minute walk of many communities. Several student-focused complexes (The Flats on University, The Main on University, and some private room rentals) advertise free CUE bus rides to GMU.
- George Mason University provides free shuttle buses to the nearest Metro station in Vienna. The campus is accessible from I-66 and I-495.

Picking a side of campus:
- Students attending the Volgenau School of Engineering may prefer the south side of campus near Braddock Road.
- Students at the School of Business may prefer the north side near University Drive.

Neighborhoods and areas students consider:
- Fairfax City is closest to campus and includes communities like Layton Hall, Fairfax Square, eaves Fairfax City, and The Point at Fairfax.
- Fairfax Station (e.g., Oakton Park) and the area near campus include GMU-affiliated Masonvale.
- Centreville, Vienna, and Manassas are common for cheaper rent but come with a longer commute (roughly 25 to 30-plus minutes). Rooms in private homes in these areas can run $500-$900 per month.

Cost context:
- The average rent for an apartment near George Mason University is about $2,680 per month, though cheaper rooms and farther-out apartments are available for less.

Finding listings:
- GMU's official Off-Campus Housing Marketplace (och.gmu.edu) lets students filter rentals by price, bedrooms, distance, pet policy, safety features, and amenities, and sort by closest to campus. The university also offers peer mentors to help students search.
