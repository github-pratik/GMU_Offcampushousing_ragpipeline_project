---
Fairfax Square is a large apartment community about 7 minutes from George Mason University. Rent runs roughly $1,995 to $2,987 per month. Reviews are sharply mixed.

What residents praise:
- Staff and neighbors are often described as kind and helpful, and the community continually looks to improve its services.
- The maintenance team is frequently called quick to address issues and willing to go above and beyond.
- Some residents highlight soundproof walls, cleanliness, good value compared to the metro-area average, and a pet-friendly atmosphere.

What residents complain about:
- One resident reported sewage spewing from their sink for months, with repeated requests ignored and no rent proration offered. The same resident noted management became responsive only when claiming the resident had violated lease terms.
- Another resident called it "the worst experience in the DMV," citing misleading listing photos, slow responses, and being charged about $2,000 for a month outside their lease because they gave insufficient notice.
- Complaints commonly center on billing/move-out charges and inconsistent responsiveness depending on the issue.
