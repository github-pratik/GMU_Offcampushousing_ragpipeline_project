Layton Hall Apartments sits at 10320 Layton Hall Drive in Fairfax City and is rated about 3.2 out of 5 stars from 67 resident votes. Rent runs roughly $1,763 to $1,973 per month, among the cheapest in Fairfax.

What residents praise:
- Excellent location, close to many stores and activities.
- Some of the cheapest rent in Fairfax City.
- Parking is reported as great, with good visitor parking available.
- Some residents report maintenance has been efficient.

What residents complain about:
- Pests are a major recurring theme: residents warn of cockroaches, mice, and bedbugs. One resident reported living there four years with bugs, roaches, and ants, needing pest control almost every two weeks.
- Staff is rated very low (around 1 out of 5) for friendliness, responsiveness, and helpfulness.
- It is an old building, leading to noise issues and poor construction standards.
- Broken amenities such as washing machines and heating systems come up repeatedly.
- Grounds and upkeep of common areas are rated low (around 2 out of 5).
