Oakton Park Apartments is located in Fairfax Station and managed by Dreyfuss Management. Note a data tension worth flagging: some listing aggregators show a high overall star rating, but the detailed written reviews are considerably more critical.

What residents complain about:
- Management and communication: residents describe the leasing office staff as rude and say they do not take time to understand concerns. Experiences with specific staff members vary, but communication is a common theme.
- Pests: multiple residents report the buildings are "absolutely infested with German cockroaches." Residents say they put in tickets repeatedly but only received individual-apartment spraying rather than a building-wide fix.
- Parking: described as "absolutely inexcusable," with residents competing for spots ("rat races"), guests at risk of being towed, and people having to park offsite and walk when no spots remain.
- Noise: residents report hearing kids and adults running up and down hallways and stairs at all hours. Units near the playground hear children playing from 7am until well after dark, often unsupervised.

What residents praise:
- The apartments themselves are spacious, utilities are included, and the location is convenient. Some residents report responsive maintenance staff in specific cases.
