Masonvale is a George Mason University-affiliated apartment community on the east side of the Fairfax campus, popular with graduate students and staff for its short commute.

What residents praise:
- The maintenance team is described as friendly, chill, and quick to fix things.
- The community feels safe and quiet, and is very convenient to George Mason University.
- Units are spacious and well built (granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, high ceilings), and rent is competitively priced below the market average.

What residents complain about:
- Heat and AC policies are a recurring frustration: upstairs rooms get cold in winter and hot in summer.
- Leases are one year and are not guaranteed to be renewed, with a maximum stay of about three years. Many residents have asked management to change this policy, but report that management has not addressed it.
- One resident said they were forced out at the end of their lease because they "weren't high enough up on their chain of who deserves a home."
- Several residents describe the leasing office as "shady," summing it up as wonderful units but "horrible experiences with the office."
